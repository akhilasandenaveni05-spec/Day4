Smart Hostel Management Platform

Traditional hostel management is often handled manually, making it difficult to manage room allocation, student complaints, leave requests, notices, and mess feedback efficiently. A Smart Hostel Management System can provide a centralized online platform where students and hostel administrators can manage these activities easily, reducing paperwork and improving communication.
